What Gets Passed Down Wisdom, objects, stories, silence?

What we noticed: Some knowledge travels easily (food, jokes). Other knowledge — about regret, fear, or unfinished dreams — often stays unspoken. This is one of the questions we bring to our guest.

Growing Older vs Growing Up Is the shift about age or about responsibility?
Strong expectation of care for parents; identity tied to family role.
Emphasis on individual autonomy; “age is just a number” vs. ageism in work.
